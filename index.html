02 / MY STORY

People first.
Always learning.

Health is personal.
So is the work of improving it.

I’m a public health professional based in Salt Lake City, with a focus on health equity and support for children and families. I joined Centro de la Familia in 2019 and earned my Master of Public Health from Utah State University in 2025.

I’m interested in the connection between the big picture and everyday life: how a program is organized, how information is shared, and how practical tools can help people move forward.
