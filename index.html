03 / MY STORY

Rooted in people.
Driven by curiosity.

I’m interested in the people behind the numbers—and the ideas that help them thrive.

My journey started in community outreach and grew into health and nutrition leadership. I joined Centro de la Familia in 2019, and I earned my Master of Public Health from Utah State University in 2025. That experience continues to shape the way I think about service, education, and opportunity.

I’m also the kind of person who enjoys discovering a useful app, experimenting with AI, or rethinking a process that could be simpler. I believe good technology should support people—not get in their way.

Based in Salt Lake City, I’m always learning, connecting ideas, and looking for practical ways to make things better.
